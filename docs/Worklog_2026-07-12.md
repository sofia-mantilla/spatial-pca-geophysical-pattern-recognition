Sofia recalled the corrected fusion *decreased* performance, matching a reference figure. Findings:

1. **The reference figure exists verbatim** at
   `outputs/Output_Carajas_Brazil_Multivariate_TMI_U_clean_sweep_current_20260514/_validation_comparisons/with_raw_multi_by_training_deposit/Training_Deposit_3_spca_uni_tmi_uni_u_raw_multi_spca_multi.png`
   (created **2026-05-14 17:24**, from the clean-sweep pkls as they existed that day). Values: raw multi 0.3%; sPCA-TMI k=2 31.0%; sPCA-U k=34 52.8%; **sPCA multi k_fused=6 49.3%, hits deposits 1, 2, 4**.
2. **Those multi pkls no longer exist**: the Deposit-3 multi runs in the clean-sweep folder were regenerated **2026-05-16 14:30** (commit `0a3a12f` "Added raw cases", which touched `pipeline.py` (292 lines) and `ranking.py` (+64)). The rerun gives **70.4%, 4 hits** — different result, same config.
3. **Same commit, different runs, different fused results** = the tied-eigenvalue basis nondeterminism documented in `docs/reproducibility_debug_deposit3_multivariate.md` (fused PCA eigenvalues ≈1.0000758 are tied; the retained-PC basis can rotate between runs; deposit weights and distances change with it). The univariate runs are unaffected (stable across reruns) — only the two-stage fusion path is nondeterministic.
4. **Surviving corrected artifact:** `Output_..._Square_TMI2_U34/Deposit_3_..._kpcs_6` (2026-05-14 08:26, commit `548bb6b`): end 49.2%, AUC 92.7 (mean-fraction × 250 convention), hits deposits 1@94, 2@150, 4@80 — consistent with the reference figure.
5. **The 77.7% / AUC 99.22 in the v9 tracked insertion matches no surviving run** — most plausibly another draw of the nondeterministic basis.

**Paper updated to:** multi endpoint 49.3%, highest AUC (92.7 vs 82.5 U / 57.3 TMI / 0.2 raw), 3 of 4 hits; narrative = fusion improves *ordering*, not endpoint. `docs/figures/multi_recovery_comparison.png` replaced with the authoritative 05-14 figure.
